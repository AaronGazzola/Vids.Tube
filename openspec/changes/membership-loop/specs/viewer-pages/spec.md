## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Credits page

**Reason**: The page never existed. The requirement describes a wallet a viewer tops up — a balance, purchasable credit packages, and a purchase history — which belongs to an earlier monetisation design that was not built. What exists instead is a credit ledger where credits are earned from participation at a fixed rate and cannot be bought, so a page offering packages for sale would describe the opposite of the system.

**Migration**: None. No route, component, or navigation entry is being removed, because none was ever created. A member's balance is surfaced on their membership card, alongside the XP it derives from. If credits become purchasable later, that is a new change with its own spec rather than a revival of this one.
